Privacy Policy

Application: Drive Backup Services (Drive Backup Service)

Developer: Drive Backup Services Engineering Team

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Drive Backup Services's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, the application requests the following restricted OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This scope grants access only to the files and folders created or opened by Drive Backup Services. It does not access any other personal files, documents, or photos in your Google Drive.

1. 100% Offline-First Architecture

Drive Backup Services is designed with an offline-first architecture. All core business records (retail sales, accounts, inventories, transaction ledgers) are stored strictly locally on your machine in an offline SQLite database. We do not operate external web servers to collect, harvest, monetize, or track your business transactions.

2. Purpose of Google Drive Integration

The Google Drive integration serves one explicit purpose: allowing users to safely backup and restore their local desktop database to their own private Google Drive storage with 1 click or on a scheduled basis. Your backup archives are encrypted locally with AES-256 before being uploaded to your Drive.

3. Data Protection and Zero Third-Party Sharing

Your authentication tokens are stored securely in your operating system's local encrypted credentials vault. We never share, sell, distribute, or transmit any Google user data or business records to advertisers or third parties.

4. User Control & Revocation

You can disconnect your Google account at any time directly within the desktop application settings, or revoke access remotely through your Google Account Security Settings.

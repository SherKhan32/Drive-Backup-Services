Privacy Policy

Application: Drive Backup Service (by Drive Backup Services — includes the Installment Management System desktop app)  •  Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment

Drive Backup Service by Drive Backup Services is an offline-first desktop software suite (Petrol Pump Management, Installment Management, Auto Parts & Decoration Inventory). All loan accounts, customer records, and transaction ledgers are stored locally on your own device in an encrypted SQLite database. We operate no backend servers that collect, store, sell, or analyze your business data or Google account information. No login is required to browse this website.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure

Drive Backup Service's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

OAuth scope requested:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This restricted scope grants access only to files created or opened by Drive Backup Service itself. The app cannot view, read, edit, or delete your photos, emails, Google Docs, or any other files in your Google Drive.

1. How we access, use, and store Google user data

  • Purpose: the sole purpose of Google Drive access is to save encrypted backup archives of your local database into your own Google Drive, only when you click backup/sync or enable scheduled backups.
  • Direct client-to-Google communication: OAuth 2.0 authorization happens in your own browser directly with Google. No proxy or intermediary server is used, and tokens never pass through our systems (we have none).
  • Local token storage: OAuth access/refresh tokens stay in your OS credential vault (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service).
  • Local encryption before upload: backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device before upload; only you hold the key.
  • What we do NOT access: Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any Drive files not created by this app.

2. Data collection, sharing, and selling

  • We do NOT collect personal data through this website (no accounts, no analytics fingerprints, no advertising trackers).
  • We do NOT sell Google user data or business ledger data to anyone.
  • We do NOT use Google user data for advertising, retargeting, credit profiling, or to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • We do NOT transfer Google user data to third parties except to Google's own Drive API endpoints as strictly needed to store the backup file you requested.
  • No human at our organization can access your database or Drive files — there is no server side.

3. Data retention and deletion (user control)

  • Local data: retained on your device until you delete the app/database file (installment_manager.db) yourself.
  • Cloud backups: retained in your Google Drive folder (/InstallmentBackups/) until you delete them in Google Drive.
  • Revoking access: disconnect inside the desktop app Settings at any time, or revoke at Google Account Security Permissions. Revocation immediately stops future uploads; it does not delete files already in your Drive (you delete those yourself).

4. Information processed locally by the software

Only to run its offline features on your device: customer name/CNIC/phone/address/guarantor details, installment contracts (principal, markup, schedule, down payment), ledger transactions (receipt numbers, collections, penalties), and receipt layouts. None of this leaves your device except inside the encrypted backup file you choose to upload to your own Drive.
